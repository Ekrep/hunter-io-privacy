Privacy Policy

Drunk Game Studio ("we", "our", or "us") built Hunter.io as an ad-supported mobile game with optional in-app purchases. We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your real name, home address, or phone number. Information collected for gameplay analytics and advertising is anonymous or pseudonymous.

How Your Device Is Identified

You may access Hunter.io on your mobile phones, tablets, and other wireless computing devices ("Device"). Drunk Game Studio does not request or record personal identity data on your Device. Game progress, settings, and your chosen in-game nickname are stored locally on your Device, and anonymous technical identifiers may be used by integrated analytics and advertising services.

Advertising

Drunk Game Studio uses third-party advertising networks and services to display rewarded and interstitial advertisements in Hunter.io. When you play Hunter.io, you allow third-party advertising networks and services to serve and deliver ads to you and your Device as part of their services.

In order to serve advertisements (including personalized advertisements), advertising networks and services may use and track the advertising ID or unique mobile identifier from the Device (for example, Android Advertising ID (AAID) or Advertising Identifier (IDFA) for iOS), IP address, and ad interaction data.

Google AdMob

Google AdMob is an advertising service provided by Google LLC. Please visit the web pages below for more information on how Google uses data:

  • Google Advertising Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Analytics & Cloud Services (Firebase)

Hunter.io uses Google Firebase Analytics and Cloud Firestore to measure gameplay performance, diagnose crashes, and improve game balance and stability. These services may collect anonymous usage events, device model, OS version, and app version.

  • Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases allow you to purchase virtual items in Hunter.io using real money. Payments are processed securely by third-party store services (Google Play Billing / Unity IAP). Drunk Game Studio does not receive or store your credit card or billing details, though we may receive transaction confirmation receipts.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Data Retention & Deletion

Local gameplay data is stored on your Device and can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Hunter.io. If you wish to request deletion of any analytics or server-side data associated with your gameplay, please contact us using the email address below.

Children's Privacy

Hunter.io is not knowingly directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.
